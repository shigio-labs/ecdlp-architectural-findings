# Reading List

The following works informed our analysis but are not redistributed
here for copyright reasons. Readers should obtain them from the
original publishers via institutional access or open versions where
available.

## Co-Z arithmetic and X-only ladders

- Goundar, Joye, Miyaji. "Co-Z Addition Formulae and Binary Ladders
  on Elliptic Curves." CHES 2010, LNCS 6225, pp. 65–79. Springer.
  https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-642-15031-9_5
  Open version: https://eprint.iacr.org/2010/309

- Goundar, Joye, Miyaji, Rivain, Venelli. "Scalar Multiplication on
  Weierstraß Elliptic Curves from Co-Z Arithmetic." Journal of
  Cryptographic Engineering 1(2):161–176, 2011. Springer.
  https://doi.org/10.1007/s13389-011-0012-0

- Hutter, Joye, Sierra. "Memory-Constrained Implementations of
  Elliptic Curve Cryptography in Co-Z Coordinate Representation."
  AFRICACRYPT 2011, LNCS 6737. Springer.

- Brier, Joye. "Weierstraß Elliptic Curves and Side-Channel Attacks."
  PKC 2002, LNCS 2274.

- Explicit-Formulas Database. "XZ coordinates for short Weierstrass
  curves." https://www.hyperelliptic.org/EFD/g1p/auto-shortw-xz.html

## Foundational ECDLP quantum work

- Roetteler, Naehrig, Svore, Lauter. "Quantum resource estimates
  for computing elliptic curve discrete logarithms." ASIACRYPT 2017.

- Häner, Jaques, Naehrig, Roetteler, Soeken. "Improved Quantum
  Circuits for Elliptic Curve Discrete Logarithms." PQCrypto 2020.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.09580

- Chevignard, Fouque, Schrottenloher. "Reducing the Number of Qubits
  in Quantum Discrete Logarithms on Elliptic Curves." EUROCRYPT 2026.
  https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/280

## RNS arithmetic and base extension

- Kawamura, Koç, Naccache, Shparlinski. "Cox-Rower Architecture for
  Fast Parallel Montgomery Multiplication." EUROCRYPT 2000.

- Bajard, Eynard, Hasan, Zucca. "A Full RNS Variant of FV like
  Somewhat Homomorphic Encryption Schemes."

## Pebbling and reversible computation

- Bennett. "Time/space trade-offs for reversible computation."
  SIAM J. Comput. 18(4):766–776, 1989.

- Knill. "An analysis of Bennett's pebble game." 1995.

- Meuli, Soeken, Roetteler, Bjorner, De Micheli. "Reversible Pebbling
  Game for Quantum Memory Management." DATE 2019.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.02121

- Gidney. "Spooky pebble games" — measurement-based uncomputation.

## Partial-information attacks (used in §4 cryptographic argument)

- Howgrave-Graham, Smart. "Lattice attacks on digital signature
  schemes." Designs, Codes and Cryptography, 2001.

- Nguyen, Shparlinski. "The insecurity of the Digital Signature
  Algorithm with partially known nonces." J. Cryptology, 2002.
